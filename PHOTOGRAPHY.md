# Sample garment photographs

These are real, separately sourced example photographs, not a retailer feed or verified colour variants of one SKU. No colour edits or background removal were performed. Large files use Wikimedia's 960px thumbnails. The leather-jacket source is already a cropped monochrome photograph; the app makes no colour changes. Human-model images are labelled in the interface. Attribution and source links are also displayed in the garment detail view. Source pages link to their applicable licenses.

## Category rail derivatives (v8)

`assets/rack/*.png` are transparent derivatives of the licensed catalogue photographs, not generated garments. Background removal uses the existing local U2NetP browser pipeline; originals and their source/licence records remain in `src/studio/closet.ts`. Mapping: tshirt→tshirt.jpg, knit→knit.jpg, shirt→catalog-shirt-45.jpg, trousers→jeans.jpg, shoes→shoes.jpg, dress→catalog-dress-44.jpg, jacket→catalog-jacket-13.jpg, skirt→catalog-skirts3-15.jpg, bag→handbag.jpg, accessory→catalog-accessory-33.jpg. The rack applies display perspective only; it is not a physical 3D garment simulation.
